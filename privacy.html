Versus

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Versus is designed with your privacy in mind. We don't collect personal information, run analytics, or track your activity. Your data stays on your devices.

Information You Provide

When you use Versus, you create and store:

  • Player names and profile photos
  • Player groups and custom rating definitions
  • Player ratings and scores
  • Game history and team results

All of this information is entered by you and stored locally on your device. We do not have access to it on any server we operate.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, Versus uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Camera and Photo Library

Versus may request access to your camera or photo library so you can set profile photos for players. Photos are stored locally on your device and in your iCloud account if sync is enabled. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

Subscriptions and Purchases

Versus Pro subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier — not your name, email, or Apple ID — to track whether your subscription is active. No payment information is shared with us or RevenueCat; Apple handles all billing.

You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and Tracking

Versus does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, device identifiers, or location information.

Third-Party Services

Versus uses only two third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) — for syncing your data across your devices
  • RevenueCat — for managing subscription status using anonymous identifiers

No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

Data Retention

Your data is stored on your device and in your iCloud account for as long as you choose to keep it. You can delete your data at any time by deleting players, groups, or game records within the app, or by deleting the app entirely.

Children's Privacy

Versus does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require an account, and all data remains on the user's device and iCloud account. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address the concern.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@redshifted.co.
